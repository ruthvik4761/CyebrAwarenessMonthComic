HOW TO PLAY

1

READ THE COMIC

The story plays out across comic pages. When you’re ready to move on, click the Click to Proceed box on the right side of the comic. Clicking anywhere else won’t move the story on.

2

MAKE THE CALL

At four key moments, the story stops and asks what you’d do. Pick one of the four options. You’ll see right away whether it was the secure choice, plus a short lesson if it wasn’t.

3

BEAT THE CLOCK

The timer starts when you press Start. At the end you’ll see your final score and your total response time.

4

CONTROLS

🔊 (top right) toggles the music. ⏭ End skips straight to the results. When you finish, you can run it again or quit.
